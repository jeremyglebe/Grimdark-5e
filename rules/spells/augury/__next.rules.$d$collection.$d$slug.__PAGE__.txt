<p><strong>Casting Time:</strong> 1 minute or Ritual<br /><strong>Range:</strong> Self<br /><strong>Components:</strong> V, S, M (specially marked sticks, bones, cards, or other divinatory tokens worth 25+ GP)<br /><strong>Duration:</strong> Instantaneous</p>
<p>You receive an omen from an otherworldly entity about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The GM chooses the omen from the Omens table.</p>
