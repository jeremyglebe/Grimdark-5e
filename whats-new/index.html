Changes to the 2024 Rules

The following Grimdark procedures replace or add to the SRD 5.2.1 baseline. Follow the linked entries for the complete rules and exceptions.

1.

Melee attacks

2024 baseline. Roll the attack against the target’s Armor Class.

Grimdark rule. The defender chooses Parry, Dodge, or Tank the Hit. Parry and Dodge oppose the attack roll. Unopposed attacks hit unless the attack die is a natural 1. The winner of an opposed exchange gains 1 Momentum; the loser clears theirs.

Melee ExchangesDefense ResponsesMomentum
2.

Armour and damage reduction

2024 baseline. Armour and shields contribute to Armor Class.

Grimdark rule. Armour contributes its fixed base AC minus 10 to Damage Negation. Shields contribute their printed AC bonus. Add Toughness: Constitution modifier + proficiency bonus − 2. Subtract Negation once from incoming non-Force damage after Resistance and other damage changes. Force bypasses Negation.

3.

Damage dice

2024 baseline. Roll each damage die once unless a specific rule allows another roll.

Grimdark rule. A damage die showing its maximum adds another die of the same size. Repeat for each additional maximum. Apply this to every damage die, including spell and bonus damage. Static modifiers do not explode.

4.

Critical hits

2024 baseline. Roll the attack’s damage dice twice, then add modifiers.

Grimdark rule. Do not double the damage dice. An attacker’s natural 20 causes a Critical Injury: roll 1d100 + Excess Damage + 10 per existing unrecovered injury. Parry and Dodge have separate natural-20 effects. Multiple triggers on one blow cause one injury per affected creature.

5.

Zero Hit Points

2024 baseline. A character at 0 Hit Points normally becomes Unconscious and makes death saving throws unless another rule applies.

Grimdark rule. At 0 Hit Points, enter Mortal Peril. Do not automatically become Unconscious or make death saves. Hit Point loss beyond 0 is Excess Damage and causes a Critical Injury. Injuries, Bleeding, and other specific effects can cause Unconsciousness or death.

Mortal Peril
6.

Bleeding

2024 baseline. The standard conditions do not include this cumulative Bleeding condition.

Grimdark rule. At the start of the turn, lose one exploding Hit Die plus Bleeding level − 1 Hit Points. At 0 Hit Points, become Unconscious. A bleeding, Unconscious creature rolls 1d20 − (Bleeding level − 1) and dies on a modified 1 or lower unless prevented. Each Bleeding level removed adds 1 Exhaustion level.

Bleeding
7.

Healing injuries

2024 baseline. Healing restores Hit Points according to the effect used.

Grimdark rule. Hit Point healing does not automatically remove a Critical Injury. Use the injury’s treatment check, recovery time, and minimum healing spell-slot level. Apply any additional requirements for missing anatomy, permanent harm, or death.

Healing Critical InjuriesTreatment and Recovery
8.

Fate

2024 baseline. There is no corresponding Fate resource.

Grimdark rule. Spend 1 Fate to prevent a character’s death; no roll is required. The point is spent permanently. At character creation, start with Fate 2 and Resolve 2, then allocate 2 more points between them. Resolve’s spending and recovery procedures remain unresolved.

Unresolved rules

Resolve expenditure and recovery, Corruption and Mutation, Raw Casting and Strain, Miscasts, Lores, Fear and Terror, career backgrounds, and general monster conversion do not yet have complete procedures. First Aid, some healing interactions, and Armour Piercing also remain undefined.
