<h3>Traits</h3>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Swarm.</em></strong> The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny raven. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.</p>
<h3>Actions</h3>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Beaks.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +4, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage, or 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied.</p>
<p><strong><em>Cacophony (Recharge 6).</em></strong> <em>Wisdom Saving Throw:</em> DC 10, one creature in the swarm's space. <em>Failure:</em> The target has the Deafened condition until the start of the swarm's next turn. While Deafened, the target also has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.</p>
