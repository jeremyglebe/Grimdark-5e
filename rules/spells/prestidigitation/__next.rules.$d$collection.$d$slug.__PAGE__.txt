3:T4ed,<p><em>Transmutation Cantrip (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)</em></p>
<p><strong>Casting Time:</strong> Action<br /><strong>Range:</strong> 10 feet<br /><strong>Components:</strong> V, S<br /><strong>Duration:</strong> Up to 1 hour</p>
<p>You create a magical effect within range. Choose the effect from the options below. If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have up to three of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time.</p>
<p><em>Sensory Effect.</em> You create an instantaneous, harmless sensory effect, such as a shower of sparks, a puff of wind, faint musical notes, or an odd odor.</p>
<p><em>Fire Play.</em> You instantaneously light or snuff out a candle, a torch, or a small campfire.</p>
<p><em>Clean or Soil.</em> You instantaneously clean or soil an object no larger than 1 cubic foot.</p>
<p><em>Minor Sensation.</em> You chill, warm, or flavor up to 1 cubic foot of nonliving material for 1 hour.</p>
<p><em>Magic Mark.</em> You make a color, a small mark, or a symbol appear on an object or a surface for 1 hour.</p>
<p><em>Minor Creation.</em> You create a nonmagical trinket or an illusory image that can fit in your hand. It lasts until the end of your next turn. A trinket can deal no damage and has no monetary worth.</p>
