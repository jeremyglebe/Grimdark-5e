Toughness

Grimdark 5e · Draft rules

Toughness = Constitution modifier + (proficiency bonus − 2)

Add Toughness to Damage Negation against every damage type except Force. Psychic damage uses ordinary Negation in this draft.

Do not round negative Toughness up to 0. A negative value reduces Negation and can increase Hit Point loss.

Example. With Constitution +3 and proficiency bonus +2, Toughness is 3. At proficiency bonus +4, the same Constitution gives Toughness 5.

Grimdark 5e material: an unofficial modification or addition to the SRD 5.2.1 baseline.
