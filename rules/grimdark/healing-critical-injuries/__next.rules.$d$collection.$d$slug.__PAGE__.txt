7:T401,<p>Restoring Hit Points does not automatically remove a Critical Injury.</p>
<ol>
<li>Read the injury's treatment, recovery, and magical-healing requirements.</li>
<li>A healing spell below the listed spell-slot threshold can restore Hit Points but cannot remove that injury.</li>
<li>A spell cast at or above the threshold can repair the injury if the spell is appropriate to the harm.</li>
<li>Apply any additional requirements in the injury result, such as surgery, a particular spell, materials, or recovery time.</li>
</ol>
<p>Result-specific requirements take precedence. Ordinary healing does not regrow missing anatomy, replace an erased mind, or restore life. Some injuries require Regenerate, Wish, or equivalent effects.</p>
<p>For nonmagical treatment, use the check and recovery time in the injury table and the <a href="/Grimdark-5e/rules/critical-injuries/using-the-critical-injury-tables/">treatment procedure</a>. Treatment DCs, recovery times, and spell-slot thresholds are provisional playtest values.</p>
