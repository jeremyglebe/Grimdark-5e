<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Adhesive (Object Form Only).</em></strong> The mimic adheres to anything that touches it. A Huge or smaller creature adhered to the mimic has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). Ability checks made to escape this grapple have Disadvantage.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Bite.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +5 (with Advantage if the target is Grappled by the mimic), reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage—or 12 (2d8 + 3) Piercing damage if the target is Grappled by the mimic—plus 4 (1d8) Acid damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Pseudopod.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +5, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) Acid damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13). Ability checks made to escape this grapple have Disadvantage.</p>
<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Shape-Shift.</em></strong> The mimic shape-shifts to resemble a Medium or Small object while retaining its game statistics, or it returns to its true blob form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed.</p>
