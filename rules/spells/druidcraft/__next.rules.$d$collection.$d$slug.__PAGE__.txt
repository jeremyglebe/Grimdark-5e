3:T401,<p><em>Transmutation Cantrip (Druid)</em></p>
<p><strong>Casting Time:</strong> Action<br /><strong>Range:</strong> 30 feet<br /><strong>Components:</strong> V, S<br /><strong>Duration:</strong> Instantaneous</p>
<p>Whispering to the spirits of nature, you create one of the following effects within range.</p>
<p><em>Weather Sensor.</em> You create a Tiny, harmless sensory effect that predicts what the weather will be at your location for the next 24 hours. The effect might manifest as a golden orb for clear skies, a cloud for rain, falling snowflakes for snow, and so on. This effect persists for 1 round.</p>
<p><em>Bloom.</em> You instantly make a flower blossom, a seed pod open, or a leaf bud bloom.</p>
<p><em>Sensory Effect.</em> You create a harmless sensory effect, such as falling leaves, spectral dancing fairies, a gentle breeze, the sound of an animal, or the faint odor of skunk. The effect must fit in a 5-foot Cube.</p>
<p><em>Fire Play.</em> You light or snuff out a candle, a torch, or a campfire.</p>
