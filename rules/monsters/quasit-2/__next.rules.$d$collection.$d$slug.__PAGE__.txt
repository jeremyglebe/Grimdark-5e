<p><strong><em>Scare (1/Day).</em></strong> <em>Wisdom Saving Throw:</em> DC 10, one creature within 20 feet. <em>Failure:</em> The target has the Frightened condition. <p><strong><em>Shape-Shift.</em></strong> The quasit shape-shifts to resemble a bat (Speed 10 ft., Fly 40 ft.), a centipede (40 ft., Climb 40 ft.), or a toad (40 ft., Swim 40 ft.), or it returns to its true form.
