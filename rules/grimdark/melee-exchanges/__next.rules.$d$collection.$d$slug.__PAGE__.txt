7:T654,<p>Resolve a melee attack as follows. This procedure replaces a melee attack roll against Armor Class.</p>
<ol>
<li>The attacker declares the target, weapon, and any options that must be chosen before rolling.</li>
<li>The defender chooses Parry, Dodge, or Tank the Hit. If the defender cannot respond, the GM uses Unopposed or Surprised as appropriate.</li>
<li>The attacker rolls <code>1d20 + the normal melee attack bonus</code>. Add any applicable Momentum. If Parrying or Dodging, the defender makes the corresponding defense roll and adds any applicable Momentum.</li>
<li>Compare the totals using <a href="/Grimdark-5e/rules/grimdark/tests-and-opposition/">Tests and Opposition</a>. Check each natural 20 for the effect defined by the chosen defense.</li>
<li>If the exchange was opposed, update <a href="/Grimdark-5e/rules/grimdark/momentum/">Momentum</a>.</li>
<li>If the attack deals damage, follow the <a href="/Grimdark-5e/rules/grimdark/damage-procedure/">Damage Procedure</a>.</li>
<li>Resolve any <a href="/Grimdark-5e/rules/grimdark/critical-injuries/">Critical Injury</a> triggered by the attack or defense that the damage procedure has not already resolved. Do not apply a second injury to the same creature from the same blow.</li>
</ol>
<p>Winning a Parry or Dodge prevents the attack's normal weapon damage. A defender does not deal ordinary weapon damage for winning unless a specific feature allows it. There is no general Riposte rule.</p>
<p>A natural 20 can trigger an injury or prevent damage independently of the opposed totals; apply the selected defense's rule before resolving damage.</p>
